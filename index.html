Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first deep plane facelift consultation?

It's recommended to bring a list of your current medications, any previous medical records, and photographs of yourself when you were younger if available. This information assists the surgeon in understanding your facial changes and overall health status.

How long does a typical deep plane facelift consultation last?

A consultation generally takes 45 minutes to an hour, allowing time for a thorough examination, discussion of your goals, and answering any questions you may have.

Will I be shown before-and-after photos during the consultation?

Yes, viewing images of previous patients helps you gauge realistic outcomes and provides insight into the surgeon’s style and capabilities.

Can I bring someone with me to the consultation?

Absolutely. Bringing a trusted friend or family member can offer emotional support and help you remember important details discussed during the consultation.

How soon can surgery be scheduled after the consultation?

If you decide to proceed with surgery, it is often possible to schedule the procedure within a few weeks, depending on the surgeon’s availability and your readiness.

Is the deep plane facelift suitable for all ages?

The procedure is typically recommended for individuals experiencing moderate to significant facial sagging, often between their 40s and 60s, but suitability is determined on a case-by-case basis during the consultation.
